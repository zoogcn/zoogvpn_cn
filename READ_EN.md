## About

**ZoogVPN** is a VPN service founded in 2013 and headquartered in Patras, Greece — outside the 14-Eyes alliance. It offers apps for Windows, macOS, iOS, Android, Linux, browser extensions, Android TV, and Amazon Fire Stick.

**Key features:** AES-256 encryption, no-logs policy, kill switch, DNS leak protection, split tunneling, ad blocker, and P2P/torrent-friendly servers. Supports WireGuard, OpenVPN, IKEv2, and L2TP/IPsec. For heavily restricted regions like China, Russia, and Iran, it offers the **Shadow protocol** to bypass firewalls.

**Plans:** Free plan with 10 GB/month across 3 locations (US, UK, Netherlands). Premium from around $2.49/month on a 2-year plan, with 100+ locations, 1000+ IPs, up to 10 devices, and a 7-day money-back guarantee.

**Streaming:** Works with Netflix, BBC iPlayer, Disney+, Hulu, and Amazon Prime Video on selected servers.
